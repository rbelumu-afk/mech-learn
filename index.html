Power Calculator

Calculate mechanical power using torque and rotational speed.

Power = 1.571 kW

Formula: P = 2πNT / 60,000, where N is RPM and T is N·m.

Engineering Diagrams

Simple labelled sketches to help understand concepts.

Simple Lever

A lever rotates about a fulcrum. In an ideal lever, effort × effort arm = load × load arm.

Stress in a Bar

Normal stress under uniform axial loading: σ = F/A, where F is force and A is cross-sectional area.

Simple Gear Pair

For ideal external gears, the gears rotate in opposite directions. Speed ratio depends on their tooth counts.
